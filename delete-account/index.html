Delete your BookNStyle account

App: BookNStyle  ·  Developer: BookNStyle LLC  ·  Last updated: September 24, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your BookNStyle account and the data associated with it, what gets deleted, what we are required to keep, and for how long.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the BookNStyle app and sign in.
  2. Tap the You tab (the rightmost tab). Professionals should open the profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the screen, past Sign out.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm when prompted. Deletion begins immediately and you are signed out.

If you cannot sign in

Email support@booknstyle.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We verify ownership of the address before acting on the request and complete verified requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following are permanently removed from our active systems:

  • Your login credentials and authentication records
  • Your name, email address, phone number, and profile details
  • Your profile photo and any portfolio or service photos you uploaded
  • Any government-issued ID photo you submitted for an age-restricted booking
  • Your messages, reviews, ratings, and favorites
  • Your saved payment methods, which are removed from our payment processor
  • For professionals: your business profile, service menu, availability, and intake forms
  • Your push-notification tokens and device identifiers

What is kept, and for how long

A limited set of records is retained because law or legitimate business need requires it. These records are kept separately from your profile and are not used to contact you or to rebuild your account.

What we keepWhyHow long
Transaction and payment records — amounts, dates, and booking fees Tax, accounting, and financial-reporting obligations Up to 7 years
Records connected to an open dispute, chargeback, or legal claim To resolve the matter and defend legal rights Until resolved, then per the periods above
Limited fraud and abuse signals To prevent a banned user from immediately re-registering Up to 2 years
Copies inside routine encrypted backups Backups are written as whole snapshots and cannot be edited individually Purged on the normal rotation, within 30 days
Aggregated or de-identified statistics Cannot be linked back to you Indefinitely

Payment records held by our payment processor, Stripe, are subject to Stripe’s own retention requirements as a regulated financial-services provider. Reviews you wrote may remain visible in anonymized form, with your name and photo removed.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You do not have to close your account to remove specific content. In the app you can delete individual photos, reviews, messages, and saved payment methods, and professionals can remove services and intake forms. To request removal of particular data, email support@booknstyle.com describing what you would like removed.

Timing

In-app deletion takes effect immediately. Backup copies age out within 30 days. Email requests are completed within 30 days of verifying your identity.

Questions

Contact support@booknstyle.com. For full details on how we handle personal information, see our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
